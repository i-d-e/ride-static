# Questionnaires

Every RIDE review answers a structured questionnaire of criteria, drawn
from one of the IDE *Criteria for Reviewing* documents. Each criterion is
answered on a boolean scale (Yes / No), and some criteria offer several
options that a review may tick together (marked *(multi)* below). The
tables show, per criteria set and per question, how many reviews answered
the question and the share that answered *Yes* across the corpus. The rare
`value="3"` anomaly (used to mark "not in scope") is excluded from the
denominators and counted separately under each table.

Where the [Charts](/data/charts/) page aggregates at the top-level section,
this page breaks the same data down to the individual question. The raw
answers are also available as machine-readable JSON in the
[full corpus dump](/api/corpus.json) under `questionnaires` per review, and
the underlying TEI lives in the
[GitHub mirror](https://github.com/i-d-e/ride).
